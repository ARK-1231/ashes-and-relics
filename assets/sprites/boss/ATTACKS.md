# Attack poses

Six 180 x 180 transparent sprites in `attacks/` are loaded once at startup. Each is selected by the boss's pending attack during windup and held for 0.25 seconds after release. Idle uses the existing spellblade image. These are individual poses, not multi-frame animations.

Order in the generated sheet: melee, fan, ring / lance, spiral, wave. Colors match the game's attack warnings: red, violet, pink, cyan, orange, gold.

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original: `attacks_source.png`. Background removed with `tools/remove_boss_background.ps1`; `tools/split_boss_attacks.ps1` groups connected artwork before fitting each pose into a transparent frame, avoiding cutting weapons at cell boundaries.

## Generation prompt

Create a production RPG attack-pose SPRITE SHEET of the exact same royal spellblade boss in the reference: crown helmet, dark iron armor, burgundy cloak and chipped greatsword. Exactly SIX full-body sprites in a strict 3-column by 2-row equal-cell grid, landscape 1536x1024 canvas; every cell 512x512 with 40px empty padding. All characters same body scale, feet on same baseline within their cell, seen slightly from above facing viewer. Distinct active poses, not six identical standing poses. Row 1 left: MELEE, twisting body and sweeping greatsword sideways with a small red slash arc. Row 1 middle: FAN, extending open hand forward with five small violet magic shards fanning out just above the hand, sword lowered. Row 1 right: RING, both arms spread with a compact pink-magenta magical halo around waist, sword pointing outward. Row 2 left: LANCE, leaning forward pointing sword directly forward with a short concentrated cyan spear of energy along blade. Row 2 middle: SPIRAL, raising casting hand high with compact orange spiral energy curling around forearm, cloak twisting. Row 2 right: WAVE, crouching and slamming sword tip downward with a short golden shockwave crescent near feet. These are game character poses, NOT illustrations of large area-of-effect attacks: keep all effects small and entirely inside each cell and never crossing cells. Preserve detailed pixel-art-like dark medieval aesthetic and readable silhouettes. Each sprite complete including crown boots sword effects. No labels, no borders, no text, no floor or scenery. Actual transparent background preferred; if impossible use uniform pure white background, never checkerboard.
